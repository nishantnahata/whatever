Beer Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Summary

Beer Tracker does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you log stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The drinks you log (date and time, size, strength and an optional beer name) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are used only to show your totals, history and home-screen widget.

What the app sends

Nothing. The app has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data.

Backups you make

If you use Export, the app writes a backup file to the place you choose, such as your device storage or a cloud drive. That file is then handled by the place you saved it to, under its own terms. Beer Tracker never sees or uploads it. Import only reads the file you pick.

Android device backup

If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account settings. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Delete individual drinks in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone.

Children

Beer Tracker is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
